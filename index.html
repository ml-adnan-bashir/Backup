Equations I Live By

The mathematical foundations that power modern machine learning

∇

Gradient Descent Update

θt+1 = θt − α · ∇θ J(θ)

The workhorse of deep learning — iteratively stepping toward minima in loss landscapes.

𝒫

Bayes' Theorem

P(A|B) = P(B|A) · P(A) / P(B)

The bridge between prior beliefs and observed evidence. The foundation of probabilistic ML.

𝐻

Cross-Entropy Loss

ℒ = −Σ yi log(ŷi)

Measures the gap between predicted distributions and truth — the loss function behind classification.

D

KL Divergence

DKL(P‖Q) = Σ P(x) log(P(x)/Q(x))

Quantifies how one distribution diverges from another. Central to VAEs and information theory.

𝑄𝐾

Scaled Dot-Product Attention

Attention(Q,K,V) = softmax(QKT/√dk)V

The equation that sparked the Transformer revolution. "Attention is all you need."

ℰ

ELBO (VAE Objective)

ℒ = 𝔼q[log p(x|z)] − DKL(q(z|x)‖p(z))

Balances reconstruction fidelity with latent-space regularity in variational autoencoders.
